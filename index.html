v1-tts

JavaScript port of SAM (Software Automatic Mouth), a speech synthesizer released for the Commodore 64 in 1982. Runs entirely in the browser. Cyrillic input is transliterated before synthesis.

pitch 64
speed 72
mouth 128
throat 128
